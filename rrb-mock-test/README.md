# RRB Free Mock Tests

A free, single-file mock test website for Indian Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) exams. Open `index.html` in any browser. It needs no server, no sign-up and no build step.

## What it does

- **Full-length mocks** with the CBT section split:
  - **RRB NTPC CBT-1**: 100 questions, 90 minutes (GA 40, Maths 30, Reasoning 30)
  - **RRB Group D CBT**: 100 questions, 90 minutes (Maths 25, Reasoning 30, Science 25, GA 20)
  - **RRB ALP CBT-1**: 75 questions, 60 minutes (Maths 20, Reasoning 25, Science 20, GA 15)
- **Sectional practice**: 20 questions in 20 minutes for any one subject.
- **An exam-style screen**: countdown timer with auto-submit, *Save & next*, *Mark for review & next*, *Clear response*, and a colour-coded question palette.
- **RRB marking**: +1 for a correct answer, −1/3 for a wrong one.
- **Results**: score, accuracy, time used, a section-wise breakdown, and a worked solution for every question, with filters for wrong and unattempted questions.
- **Fresh questions each time**: maths and reasoning questions are generated at random (percentages, SI/CI, profit & loss, trains, time & work, series, coding-decoding, directions, calendars and more). GA and science questions come from a bank of static facts.
- **Attempt history**: your last 10 scores are saved in your browser's local storage.

## Hosting

The site is one static file, so any static host can serve it. For GitHub Pages, enable Pages for this repository and open `/rrb-mock-test/`.

Exam patterns change. Check the official notification on your regional RRB website before your exam.
